## Scope

Beasy web simulation (`prototype/index.html`): the whole phone app in one frame, mode Operate. Audience: Indian SMB owners and staff, plus their customers. Task: see every flow end to end, including onboarding, business registration and verification, personal vs business accounts, products, payments with escrow, shipping, AI agent, one-to-one chat from both sides, and a trust profile. All data synthetic and labelled.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every business document is a coded colour chip, like a shop's shade card, so a chat's state reads at a glance. Refuses the green-bubble messenger and the indigo SaaS dashboard.

OWN-WORLD: Warm white card stock ground (#FBFAF7), ink #1A1714. Flat solid chips, no gradients, glass or grain. Indigo #2B3A8C is the single tint for interaction. Turmeric #E8A317 is reserved for "waiting on you" only. Leaf #2E7D4F = paid/done, madder #B8322A = overdue/error. Each party owns one chip colour. Roboto/system UI on a strict scale; Barlow Condensed only on shade codes (INV 0148); Roboto Mono for rupee amounts.

STORY: The owner opens Beasy, sees what waits on them, acts inside the chat it belongs to, and trusts every counterparty through a verified profile.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Large-title "Chats", a turmeric "waiting on you" bar, a four-column strip of the most urgent document chips, filter chips, then chat rows each ending in that party's chip dots and balance; Material-style bottom navigation with four sections.

FORM: Shade card chips as the signature move: document cards in chat wear a solid status chip with a condensed code; the chat header carries a strip of chips for open documents; Pending is a four-column chip grid. Candidate 6 of 7 on the ordered list. seed:5b7d1e3526623a83

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
